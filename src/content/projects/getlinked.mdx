problem: "Hiring and job search both fail in the same way: too much noise, too little signal. Employers cannot scan a pile of profiles quickly, and candidates cannot tell which openings actually fit."
approach: "Getlinked is a two-sided product: talent builds a profile and looks at openings; hiring managers search, review documents, and reach out. The work was to make that loop obvious instead of burying it in a generic jobs board."
results: "The live app covers profile creation, job posts, search, and the hire / get-hired split, with a public site explaining the matching pitch."
lessons: "Two-sided marketplaces collapse when one side's first session is unclear. Recruiters and candidates do not share a mental model, so the UI has to name which side you are on immediately."
next: "I would cut duplicate marketing copy and make the first action after signup unmistakable: complete a profile, or post a role; not both at once."

[Getlinked](https://app.getlinked.ai/) is a matching product for talent and employers. My work sat on making the two paths, get hired and hire, readable as separate jobs, not one dashboard pretending to serve both.
