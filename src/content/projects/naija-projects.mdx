problem: "Final year projects were scattered across drives, group chats, and departmental shelves. Students looking for prior work had no reliable place to search by university, department, or topic."
approach: "I worked on a public archive where students can browse, search, and upload projects, with university and department as first-class filters rather than afterthoughts."
results: "The product is a searchable repository with featured work, university pages, and upload and citation flows aimed at making existing research easier to find and reuse."
lessons: "Archive products live or die on search and metadata. If browsing by institution and department is clumsy, the collection might as well be a shared folder."
next: "I would invest earlier in review quality and citation tools, and I would treat empty search results as a product problem, not a data problem."

[NaijaProjects](https://inter-uni-projects.vercel.app/) is a repository of undergraduate final year projects from universities across Nigeria. The idea is simple: make peer research findable, so students can learn from work that already exists instead of starting from a blank document.
